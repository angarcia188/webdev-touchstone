About Us

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make honest, beautiful bread for the neighbors who walk by each morning. Founded in 2012 by baker and longtime Tucson resident John Baker, the bakery started with one oven, a handwritten menu board, and a commitment to slow, thoughtful baking. What began as a neighborhood staple quickly became a gathering place for families, commuters, and local event planners who wanted food made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe the best bakery products start with the best ingredients. Whenever possible, we source flour from regional mills, fruit from nearby orchards, and dairy from small-batch producers. We prioritize organic grains, seasonal ingredients, and suppliers that share our values around transparency, quality, and community support.
